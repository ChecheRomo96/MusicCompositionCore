Detailed Description

Key signatures, keys in the seven diatonic modes and spelling in a key.

MCC::KeySignature stores zero to seven sharps or flats as a position on the circle of fifths. MCC::Key pairs a tonic with an MCC::KeyMode and derives its signature, so E minor has one sharp and D Dorian has none; Key::FromSignature() goes the other way. Key::Spell() writes a pitch class, or a sounding chromatic index, in the key's context: diatonic notes use the key's letters, and chromatic notes are raised in sharp keys and lowered in flat keys. See Music-Domain Specification (SPEC-KEY-*).

const MCC::Key key(MCC::NoteName(MCC::Letter::E), MCC::KeyMode::Minor);
key.Signature().Sharps(); // 1
key.Spell(MCC::PitchClass(3)); // D# (sharp key)
MCC::Key(MCC::NoteName(MCC::Letter::F), MCC::KeyMode::Major)
.Spell(MCC::PitchClass(3)); // Eb (flat key)
