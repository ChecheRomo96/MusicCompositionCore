Detailed Description

1-based interval number: 1 unison, 3 third, 8 octave, 10 tenth (SPEC-INT-2, SPEC-INT-3).

Numbers range from 1 to Maximum, the widest span between two writable pitches. Default construction and out-of-range input produce the single invalid value (SPEC-ERR-2, SPEC-ERR-3).
