Detailed Description

Directed interval: a diatonic step count and a chromatic semitone count (SPEC-INT-1).

Both counts are signed. The direction follows the diatonic steps, or the semitones for a unison, so Cb from C is a descending augmented unison and no diminished unison exists (SPEC-INT-2, SPEC-INT-5). Number and quality are derived from the magnitudes: an ascending major third is +2 steps and +4 semitones; a descending one is -2 and -4.

An interval is valid when its counts lie within the span of writable pitches and its quality is perfect, major, minor, or augmented/diminished at most four times (SPEC-INT-4). Default construction and every other input produce the single invalid value (SPEC-ERR-1..3).

Creates the interval of quality and number in direction, for example Interval(IntervalQuality::Major(), IntervalNumber(3)).

The result is invalid when the quality does not apply to the number (a perfect third, a major fifth), for a diminished unison (SPEC-INT-5), for direction Unison with anything but a perfect unison, or when either argument is invalid. A perfect unison ignores direction.
