Detailed Description

Tuning reference: a written pitch and its frequency in hertz.

Tuning::Standard() is A4 = 440 Hz (SPEC-OCT-2). Default construction, an invalid reference pitch or a frequency that is not a finite positive number produce the single invalid value (SPEC-ERR-2, SPEC-ERR-3).
